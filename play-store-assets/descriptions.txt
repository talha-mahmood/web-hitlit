FULL DESCRIPTION (816/4000 chars):
PlayOye is your all-in-one app for local sports — find games, book venues, run tournaments, and play with a squad of your own.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Discover and join sports games happening near you
- Create your own games and invite players
- Book sports venues and courts directly through the app
- Organize and run tournaments with knockout, league, or pool formats
- Build a squad with your regular teammates and register together for tournaments and games
- Chat with your squad and coordinate matches
- Track your game history and player stats

WHO IT IS FOR
Whether you play cricket, football, badminton, pickleball, or any other sport, PlayOye helps you find people to play with, venues to play at, and tournaments to compete in — all from one place.

Join PlayOye today and never struggle to find a game again.
