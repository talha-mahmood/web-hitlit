# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the **HitLit** app for mobile devices (Android and iOS), together with any related services, operated by **Hamza Mahmood** (collectively, the "Application"). Hamza Mahmood is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider."

**Effective date:** August 23, 2026

---

## Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This includes:

- Basic connection information inherent to any network request, such as your device's IP address, made when the Application communicates with its backend
- Your mobile device's operating system and general device type, used to ensure the Application functions correctly on your device

The Application does **not** use dedicated analytics tools to track which screens you visit, how long you spend on them, or your in-app behavior over time.

---

## Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application does **not** use cookies, tracking pixels, third-party analytics SDKs, or advertising SDKs of any kind. It does not build advertising profiles and is not ad-supported.

---

## Location Information

With your permission, the Application accesses your device's precise or approximate location for a single specific purpose: to help auto-fill a location when you create or join a squad.

- **Purpose-limited use:** Location data is used only for this feature — it is not used for advertising, analytics, or building a history of your movements.
- **Foreground only:** The Application does not access or track your location in the background or when the Application is closed.
- **Your control:** Location access is optional. You can decline the permission or revoke it at any time in your device settings; some convenience features may work less smoothly without it.
- **No third-party sharing of location:** Your location data is stored on the Service Provider's backend infrastructure (see "Third Party Access" below) and is not shared with advertising or analytics third parties.

---

## Visibility to Other Users

The Application is a social, sports-coordination app. As a core part of how it functions:

- Your name, profile photo, skill level, and participation in public games, tournaments, and squads are visible to other users of the Application.
- Messages you send in a game or squad chat are visible to other members of that same game or squad while it exists.
- **Chat messages are automatically deleted after 24 hours** and are not retained by the Service Provider beyond that window.
